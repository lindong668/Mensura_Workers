How to Propagate Pothos in Water: Step-by-Step Guide

Published 2026-10-07 17:47 · Elena Marsh · Photo sources

You want to root a pothos cutting in a jar but previous cuttings went black at the stem end. Take a cutting with at least one node, keep only the node submerged, and change the water until roots are a few centimetres long. The sequence matters here: diagnose first, then act once, then leave the plant alone long enough to respond.

Why This Works

Pothos roots arise from pre-formed adventitious root primordia at the node, the same structures that let it climb and attach in nature. A leaf blade has no such tissue, so node selection determines success entirely. In water, the limiting factor is dissolved oxygen: stagnant, fouled water lets soft tissue break down instead of rooting. Water-formed roots are anatomically different from soil roots, which is why the transition to mix needs a gently moist bridge rather than an immediate dry-down.

  • Do not submerge leaves or long lengths of stem.
  • Do not leave the same water unchanged for weeks.
  • Do not put the jar in direct sun.
  • Do not pot up a cutting with only one tiny root.

Daily Care Tips

Once the immediate problem is handled, these small habits keep pothos in water from drifting back into the same situation. Fold them into the watering round so A clean glass jar — gets checked at the same time.

  • Use a clear jar so you can watch root progress without disturbing it.
  • Top up as water evaporates so the node never dries.
  • Label the cutting date; two to four weeks is normal.
  • Take several cuttings at once in case one fails.

FAQs

Can I root a pothos leaf without a node?

No. A leaf alone has no meristem to produce roots. Always include a node.

How long does rooting take?

Typically two to four weeks in bright indirect light with regular water changes.

Why did my cutting turn black?

Usually submerged foliage rotting in unchanged water, or a stem buried too deep.

Should I move it to soil eventually?

Yes for long-term vigour. Keep the mix lightly moist for a fortnight while the roots adapt.

Final Thoughts

Cut with a node, keep only the node submerged, change the water, and pot up once roots are branched.
