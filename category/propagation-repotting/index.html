Propagation & Repotting

Water and soil cuttings, division, node cuttings, leaf propagation and repotting without setback.

Each guide below is written around one concrete problem: what you can see on the plant, what is happening in the pot, and what to do next. Every page gives the full answer in its initial HTML, with a materials list, ordered steps, mistakes to avoid, routine tips and short FAQs.

Guide 1 · 7 sections

How to Propagate Pothos in Water: Step-by-Step Guide

Take a cutting with at least one node, keep only the node submerged, and change the water until roots are a few centimetres long.

Guide 2 · 7 sections

How to Propagate Snake Plant: 3 Easy Methods

Division is fastest and keeps variegation; leaf cuttings work but lose variegation; water rooting is the least reliable.

Guide 3 · 7 sections

How to Divide a Peace Lily: Step-by-Step Repotting Guide

Divide at the natural crown separations in spring, keep roots attached to each section, and water well after repotting.

Guide 4 · 7 sections

Best Time to Repot Houseplants: Complete Timing Guide

Repot in spring to early summer when active growth can rebuild roots; the exceptions are rot, a failing mix or a badly root-bound plant.

Guide 5 · 7 sections

How to Propagate Monstera: Node Cutting Guide

A monstera cutting must include a node with an axillary bud; a leaf alone has no growing point.

Guide 6 · 7 sections

How to Repot a Root Bound Plant: Step by Step

Loosen the circling root mass, move one pot size up, and relearn the watering routine in the new volume.

Guide 7 · 7 sections

How to Grow Plants From Cuttings in Soil: General Guide

Use a loose, barely moist medium, keep humidity around the leaf, and avoid both stagnant water and a full dry-down.

Guide 8 · 7 sections

How to Propagate Succulents From Leaves: Full Tutorial

Only a plump, cleanly detached leaf with its base intact will root; keep it dry-ish and bright until a plantlet forms.

Guide 9 · 7 sections

Do You Water Plants After Repotting? Correct Way

For most foliage plants, water once thoroughly to settle the mix; for succulents and any cut roots, wait several days.

Guide 10 · 7 sections

How to Separate Baby Spider Plants: Propagation Guide

Root the plantlet while still attached, or cut it once it has small nubs and keep it barely moist until established.
