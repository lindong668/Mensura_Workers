Common Houseplants

Pothos, monstera, snake plant, peace lily, ZZ, spider plant, rubber plant, areca palm, fiddle leaf fig and calathea — the foliage plants most people keep indoors.

Each guide below is written around one concrete problem: what you can see on the plant, what is happening in the pot, and what to do next. Every page gives the full answer in its initial HTML, with a materials list, ordered steps, mistakes to avoid, routine tips and short FAQs.

Guide 1 · 7 sections

How to Fix Yellow Leaves on Pothos: Common Causes and Solutions

Most yellow pothos leaves come from consistently wet soil, not from thirst. Check the soil before changing anything else.

Guide 2 · 7 sections

How Often to Water a Monstera Deliciosa (Complete Guide)

Water a monstera when the top five to eight centimetres of mix are dry, not on a schedule. In most homes that lands somewhere between seven and fourteen days.

Guide 3 · 7 sections

How to Make Snake Plant Grow Faster: Pro Care Tips

Snake plant growth is limited by light and pot-bound roots far more often than by water or fertiliser. Increase brightness first.

Guide 4 · 7 sections

Why Is My Peace Lily Drooping? How to Revive It Fast

A dramatic droop is usually a water-balance problem: either a genuinely dry root ball or roots too damaged by previous overwatering to take water up.

Guide 5 · 7 sections

How to Care for a ZZ Plant: Low Light Beginner Guide

A ZZ plant needs very little: bright-to-low indirect light, a complete dry-down between waterings, and a pot that drains.

Guide 6 · 7 sections

How to Get Rid of Brown Tips on Spider Plant Leaves

Brown tips on chlorophytum usually come from dissolved salts, irregular watering or very dry air — not from a disease.

Guide 7 · 7 sections

How to Grow Rubber Plant Bushy: Pruning and Care Tips

A rubber plant branches only after the growing tip is removed or damaged; pruning in active growth is the whole mechanism.

Guide 8 · 7 sections

Best Light Conditions for Areca Palm Indoors

Areca palm wants bright indirect light all day: enough to read by, never direct midday sun on the fronds.

Guide 9 · 7 sections

How to Save Overwatered Fiddle Leaf Fig: Step by Step

Stop watering, get air back to the roots, and remove rot before it reaches the stem. Recovery is measured in new buds, not in old leaves.

Guide 10 · 7 sections

How to Keep Calathea Leaves From Curling: Care Guide

Curling is a water-balance and humidity signal: keep the mix evenly lightly moist, raise local humidity, and remove salts.

Guide 11 · 7 sections

How to Care for Chinese Evergreen (Aglaonema) Indoors: Beginner Guide

Aglaonema tolerates low light and irregular watering; keep it warm, away from drafts, and let the top soil dry between drinks.

Guide 12 · 7 sections

How to Grow a Trailing Philodendron: Long Vines Indoors

Trailing philodendrons vine fastest with bright indirect light, a drying root zone, and a support or hanging basket; pruning keeps them bushy instead of bare.
