Area Converter

Convert area across ten units from square millimetres to square miles. All factors derive from exact length definitions, squared.

Area units square their length errors

Because area is length squared, every conversion factor is the square of the corresponding length factor. A foot is 0.3048 m, so a square foot is 0.3048² = 0.09290304 m² exactly. This also means small length errors grow: a 1% error in a measurement becomes roughly a 2% error in the area.

Acres and hectares: the two land standards

An acre is 4046.8564224 m² — historically the area a team of oxen could plough in a day, later fixed as 43,560 square feet. A hectare is exactly 10,000 m², a clean metric construct of one square hectometre. One hectare is about 2.471 acres.

Why listings confuse buyers. US property is quoted in acres or square feet, most of the world in hectares or square metres. A "2-hectare" plot sounds small to an American ear but is nearly 5 acres — close to five US football fields.

Square feet versus "feet square"

These are different statements. "100 square feet" is an area of 100 ft². "100 feet square" describes a shape 100 ft on each side, which is 10,000 ft². Listings, floor plans and material estimates frequently blur the two, and the error is a factor of 100.

Practical rounding for materials

When converting area for flooring, turf or paint, round up rather than to nearest. Coverage estimates assume ideal conditions, and most materials are sold in fixed pack sizes that do not divide evenly into your exact area.
