About Mensura

Mensura is an independent collection of precision measurement and calculation tools, built to answer a specific frustration: most converters return a number without saying which standard produced it.

What we do differently

  • Definitions are stated, not hidden. Where a unit has competing standards, the page says which one is used and why.
  • Exact factors. Conversions use exact international definitions — an inch is 25.4 mm, a pound is 453.59237 g — rather than rounded shortcuts.
  • Traps are explained. The US and Imperial gallon differ by 20%. Storage vendors use decimal units while operating systems use binary. A "cup" ranges from 200 to 250 mL. These are documented on the relevant pages.
  • Privacy by design. Calculations run in your browser. No account, and your inputs are not uploaded.

Who it is for

Students checking homework, engineers sanity-checking a figure, travellers decoding foreign units, developers debugging encoding — anyone who needs a number they can trust and an explanation they can check.
