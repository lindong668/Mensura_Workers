Mensura provides free unit converters and calculators for general information and everyday convenience. This page explains the limits of what the results mean and where they should not be relied on. It should be read together with our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

1. General

Results are generated in your browser from published conversion factors and standard formulas, which we document on the methodology page. We work to keep them accurate, but no automated tool is guaranteed to be correct in every case, and we make no warranty that any result is fit for a particular purpose.

2. Not professional advice

Nothing on this site constitutes legal, medical, financial, engineering or other professional advice. This matters most where a number has real consequences:

  • Health and medicine — body-weight, body-mass and dosage-adjacent conversions are informational only. Do not use them to determine medication doses; follow the guidance of a qualified clinician or pharmacist.
  • Engineering, construction and manufacturing — where tolerances matter, use certified specifications and calibrated instruments rather than a web converter.
  • Finance, tax and legal filings — currency, percentage and date results are arithmetic aids, not authoritative figures for contracts, filings or payments.
  • Shipping, aviation and freight — weight and volume limits are set by the carrier; always confirm against the carrier's own published allowance before you travel or ship.

3. Standards and definitions

Where an exact definition exists we use it — for example the international avoirdupois pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg and the inch is exactly 25.4 mm. Where units share a name across systems, or where a customary unit has more than one definition (US versus Imperial gallons, dry versus liquid pints), the tool states which one it applies. Check that the variant you selected is the one your document or jurisdiction means.

Rounding is applied only for display. Where a result is shown rounded, the underlying figure is computed at full precision before rounding.
