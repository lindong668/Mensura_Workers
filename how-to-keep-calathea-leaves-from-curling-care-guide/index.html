How to Keep Calathea Leaves From Curling: Care Guide

Published 2026-10-11 13:26 · Elena Marsh · Photo sources

Calathea leaves curl inward during the day even though you watered, and the edges are starting to crisp. Curling is a water-balance and humidity signal: keep the mix evenly lightly moist, raise local humidity, and remove salts. Work in this order; each step depends on the one before it, and skipping ahead usually means undoing it later.

What You’ll Need

For calathea, the useful kit is short and ordinary:

  • Low-mineral or rainwater — for a thorough, even watering without over-soaking
  • A pebble tray or a nearby humidifier — to give the plant the right spot and conditions while it recovers
  • A free-draining but moisture-retentive mix — to keep the root zone airy and free-draining
  • A shallow basin — so water can drain freely and never sit under the roots
  • Clean scissors — for a clean cut that does not tear plant tissue

Safety note. No hazardous materials. Keep a pebble tray stable so it cannot spill onto electrics. Work on a stable surface, and keep pets and children away from cut material.

They separate a simple calathea problem from one that needs a different response entirely:

  • Leaf edges roll inward along the length of the blade.
  • Brown crispy margins develop after the curl.
  • The plant sits near a vent or in dry heated air.

Step-by-Step Guide

Work through the steps in order; each one builds on the previous, and you can stop once the plant is recovering.

  1. Step 1 — Check the mix with a finger: it should feel lightly moist, never soggy and never bone dry

    Calathea has fine roots that die back quickly once the pot fully dries, then cannot rehydrate the leaf.

  2. Step 2 — Water with low-mineral water and let the excess drain fully

    These marantaceae are salt-sensitive; tap-water minerals concentrate at the leaf margin and create the crisp edge.

  3. Step 3 — Raise humidity around the plant with a pebble tray or grouping, not by constant misting

    A stable local humidity reduces the leaf's water loss; a quick mist evaporates in minutes.

  4. Step 4 — Move it out of heater airflow and away from cold draughts

    Moving dry air strips water from the thin blade faster than the roots can replace it.

  5. Step 5 — Keep it in bright indirect light, never direct sun

    Direct beam increases transpiration and turns a mild curl into a scorched margin.

  6. Step 6 — Trim only fully brown edges and watch the next unfurling leaf

    Damaged tissue does not uncurl; the new leaf is the real test.

Why This Works

Calathea leaves are thin, broad and adapted to still, humid forest understorey. They also fold and lift at night through a pulvinus, which is normal. A persistent daytime inward curl means transpiration exceeds uptake: either the fine roots were damaged by drying, or the surrounding air is too dry. Fixing the root zone and the air together works; misting alone does not.

That is also why the common "quick fix" tends to fail on calathea: it treats the visible symptom rather than the condition producing it.

What to Avoid

Each item below reverses one of the steps you just read.

  • Do not let the pot dry out completely between waterings.
  • Do not mist repeatedly as a substitute for humidity.
  • Do not feed a curling plant to "strengthen" it.
  • Do not put it in a sunny window to help it dry.

Daily Care Tips

Once the immediate problem is handled, these small habits keep calathea from drifting back into the same situation.

  • Group it with other thin-leaved tropicals.
  • Flush the pot occasionally to move salts down and out.
  • Check the soil every few days rather than on a fixed schedule.
  • Expect some nightly leaf movement; that is normal nyctinasty, not curling.

FAQs

Why are my calathea leaves curling even though I water?

The roots may be damaged by a previous dry-down, or dry air is pulling water out faster than the roots supply it.

Is misting enough?

Usually not. Stable local humidity and correct soil moisture do more than repeated misting.

Can curled leaves flatten again?

A mildly curled leaf often relaxes once conditions improve; a crispy brown edge will not recover.

Does calathea need distilled water?

It is not mandatory, but low-mineral water noticeably reduces margin burn in hard-water areas.

Final Thoughts

Even light moisture, low-mineral water and stable humidity — then judge the next leaf, not the current one.

Where this guide applies

This applies to a plant in ordinary indoor potting mix.
