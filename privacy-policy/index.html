Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Applies to https://saoo.me (Plantorial)

1. Who we are (data controller)

Plantorial is an independent editorial website publishing practical indoor plant care guides. For the purposes of data protection law, Plantorial is the controller of the limited data described below. You can contact us about this policy, or to exercise any of your rights, at yokok8@outlook.com.

2. What we collect

Plantorial has no accounts, no sign-up, no comment system, no contact form and no upload function. We do not ask you to submit personal data in order to read a guide. The only information we receive is anonymous access log data and basic browser information generated when a page is requested — for example the requested URL, a truncated IP address, browser type, language setting, referrer and timestamp. We do not use this to identify you, and we do not actively collect names, addresses, payment details or any other directly identifying information.

Our hosting provider may keep standard access logs for security and delivery. Those logs are processed on our behalf and are not combined with advertising data by us.

3. Legal bases for processing

We rely on legitimate interests for the basic operation of the site: serving pages, keeping them secure, and understanding aggregate traffic so we can tell which guides are useful. Where advertising personalisation is involved, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the opt-out routes below. We do not process special-category data and we do not make automated decisions about individuals.

4. Cookies and advertising

This site is operated with the intention of displaying third-party advertising, including Google AdSense. When ad code is live on a page, third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons to collect information as a result of ad serving. Google's advertising cookies may be used to show ads based on a user's prior visits to this site and to other websites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and you can learn about or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. If no ad code is present on a page, no advertising vendor is called from that page. For visitors in the EEA and the UK, non-essential advertising cookies require consent before they are set; where a consent mechanism is deployed, it controls that behaviour, and this paragraph describes the disclosure rather than replacing the consent choice.

5. Your GDPR rights

If you are in a jurisdiction that grants them, you have the following eight rights in relation to any personal data we hold:

  1. Right of access — to ask whether we hold data about you and to receive a copy of it.
  2. Right to rectification — to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  3. Right to erasure — to ask for data to be deleted where there is no overriding reason to keep it.
  4. Right to restrict processing — to ask us to pause processing while a dispute or accuracy question is resolved.
  5. Right to data portability — to receive data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  6. Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling related to it.
  7. Right to withdraw consent — to withdraw consent for advertising personalisation at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
  8. Right to lodge a complaint — to complain to your local supervisory authority if you believe your rights have not been respected.

To exercise any of these rights, email yokok8@outlook.com with the right you are invoking and enough detail for us to locate the relevant record. We respond within the applicable statutory period, normally within one month. Because we collect no identifying information proactively, most requests will concern access logs held by our hosting provider, and we will tell you plainly what is and is not available.

6. Sharing with third parties

We share only anonymous, non-identifying access data with service providers needed to run the site — principally hosting and, where enabled, Google AdSense and other advertising vendors. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share names, email addresses or message contents with advertisers. Any email you send us is used solely to reply to you, then deleted.

7. Data retention

Access logs are retained only for as long as needed for security, delivery and aggregate traffic review. Correspondence is kept until your question is resolved and then deleted. Advertising-related data is governed by the relevant vendor's own retention policy, not by us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the site's features or the applicable requirements change. Any update will be published on this page with a revised effective date; we will not silently reduce the rights described here. See also our Advertising Disclosure and Terms of Use.
