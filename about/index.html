About Plantorial

Expert Plant Care Tutorials for Healthy Houseplants.

Plantorial is a small independent editorial project built around one narrow job: answering the specific question a person has when an indoor plant is not doing well. General gardening sites cover gardens. Plant catalogues describe what a species looks like. Neither usually tells you which field to check first, what a symptom actually means, or what to do in the next twenty minutes.

What we publish

We publish practical, self-contained guides on everyday houseplant care, pest and disease problems, and propagation and repotting. Every guide follows the same honest structure: the materials you need, ordered steps with the reason for each one, the mistakes to avoid, routine habits, short FAQs, and a plain one-sentence conclusion. Nothing is held back for a second click, and no guide depends on a script running in your browser.

How the content is checked

Our guides are written from established horticultural basics — how roots take up water and oxygen, why a node is required for a cutting, why short-day plants need uninterrupted darkness, why a coarse mix drains faster than a fine one. These are stable principles of plant growth rather than seasonal news, so a guide written today remains useful for years. We state plainly when a rule depends on your own light, pot or climate, and we tell you to verify on the plant itself rather than on a calendar.

Who is behind it

Plantorial is not a nursery, a retailer, a government body or a professional horticultural advisory service. It is a reference site written and maintained by a small team of houseplant growers who have killed enough plants to know which advice actually helps. We are reachable at yokok8@outlook.com for corrections: if you send a dated observation that contradicts a guide, we check it and amend the page.

Transparency

About, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Advertising Disclosure and Contact are plain links in the initial HTML of every page. The site has no accounts, no forms, no upload field and no analytics script of its own. Advertising, where enabled, sits outside the guides; see the Advertising Disclosure for how that works.

About the author

Elena Marsh is the writer and reviewer behind Plantorial. She prepares practical houseplant care guides focused on the specific symptom a person sees at home: the likely cause, the signs that separate it from the opposite problem, the materials you need, ordered steps with a reason attached to each, the mistakes to avoid, follow-up care, and the questions readers actually ask. The plant facts relied on are stable horticultural basics — root-zone oxygen, node cuttings, photoperiod, porous media and water-storing tissue — not news, policy or anything that expires.

You can verify the site without an account: every page is static HTML with its text in the source; each guide links to its category and to related guides; all 66 images are listed below with their source details; and there are no logins, forms or tracking scripts. Corrections, questions and GDPR requests go to yokok8@outlook.com. When a factual error is confirmed we edit the guide in place rather than publishing a correction post.

Editorial review date: 23 November 2026. Publishing model: a fixed set of evergreen guides, reviewed on error reports rather than republished on a schedule.
