Succulents & Cacti

Echeveria, jade, Christmas cactus and indoor cacti — water-storing plants that fail from excess moisture far more often than from neglect.

Each guide below is written around one concrete problem: what you can see on the plant, what is happening in the pot, and what to do next. Every page gives the full answer in its initial HTML, with a materials list, ordered steps, mistakes to avoid, routine tips and short FAQs.

Guide 1 · 7 sections

How Often to Water Succulents Indoors: Complete Watering Guide

Water succulents only after the mix is completely dry, then soak and drain. Indoors that is typically every ten to twenty days, less in winter.

Guide 2 · 7 sections

Why Is My Succulent Turning Yellow? Causes and Fixes

Yellow translucent leaves on a succulent almost always mean excess water or insufficient light, usually both at once.

Guide 3 · 7 sections

How to Grow Echeveria: Full Care for Rosette Succulents

A compact echeveria needs strong light, a fast-drying gritty mix and water that never sits in the rosette centre.

Guide 4 · 7 sections

How to Save a Rotting Cactus: Step-by-Step Rescue Guide

Cut back to clean tissue, let the wound dry and callus, then root the healthy top in dry gritty mix.

Guide 5 · 7 sections

Best Soil Mix for Succulents: DIY Recipe and Tips

Use a mostly mineral mix that drains within seconds: roughly two parts grit to one part organic potting mix.

Guide 6 · 7 sections

How to Get a Christmas Cactus to Bloom Again

These jungle cacti set buds in response to long uninterrupted nights and cooler temperatures, not to fertiliser.

Guide 7 · 7 sections

Why Is My Succulent Stretching? How to Fix Leggy Growth

Stretching (etiolation) is a light deficit. Increase light gradually, then reset the shape by beheading and rerooting the top.

Guide 8 · 7 sections

How to Propagate Jade Plant From Leaves and Cuttings

Stem cuttings root far more reliably than single leaves; both need a dry callus before they touch damp mix.

Guide 9 · 7 sections

Best Light for Indoor Cacti: Sunlight Requirements

Most indoor cacti need several hours of strong light daily; a bright room is not the same as a sunny windowsill.

Guide 10 · 7 sections

How to Repot a Succulent Without Killing It: Beginner Guide

Repot into dry gritty mix, disturb the roots as little as needed, and wait several days before the first watering.

Guide 11 · 7 sections

Why Is My Jade Plant Shriveling? Wrinkled Leaves and How to Fix Them

Wrinkled jade leaves usually mean underwatering or an old root ball that cannot take up water. Check the soil moisture before adding anything else.
