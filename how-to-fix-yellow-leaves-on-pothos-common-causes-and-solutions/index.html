How to Fix Yellow Leaves on Pothos: Common Causes and Solutions

Published 2026-09-16 14:07 · Elena Marsh · Photo sources

A pothos that was green last month now has soft yellow leaves at the base and you cannot tell whether you watered too much or too little. Most yellow pothos leaves come from consistently wet soil, not from thirst. Check the soil before changing anything else. Start with the simplest check, because the most obvious-looking cause is often not the one at work.

Why This Works

Pothos stores water in its stems and tolerates a dry root zone far better than a permanently wet one. When soil pores stay filled, roots cannot take up oxygen, the oldest leaves are shed first, and the plant sacrifices them to keep the growing tip alive. That is why the pattern — old leaves yellow, new leaves green — points to the root zone rather than to light or feeding.

That is also why the common "quick fix" tends to fail on pothos: it treats the visible symptom rather than the condition producing it. This also explains why Empty the saucer every time, without is a follow-up habit rather than a fix.

What to Avoid

Four responses undo the work above; Do not water on a fixed calendar is the one people reach for first. Avoid these while Yellowing starts with the oldest, lowest leaves while the is still present, not only after it clears.

  • Do not water on a fixed calendar regardless of soil moisture.
  • Do not add fertiliser to a plant with active root rot.
  • Do not cut half-yellow leaves off in the hope they will recover colour.
  • Do not move a struggling pothos into direct midday sun to "dry it out".

Daily Care Tips

Once the immediate problem is handled, these small habits keep pothos from drifting back into the same situation. None of this needs a new purchase; Empty the saucer every time, without is the one people forget.

  • Empty the saucer every time, without exception.
  • Rotate the pot a quarter turn monthly so all sides get equal light.
  • Wipe the leaves occasionally so they photosynthesise efficiently.
  • Check the newest leaf, not the oldest, when judging whether the fix is working.

FAQs

Can yellow pothos leaves turn green again?

No. A fully yellow leaf has already been abandoned by the plant. Judge recovery by the next two new leaves instead.

Is yellowing always overwatering?

Usually, but low nitrogen, cold draughts and a root-bound pot produce a similar look. Check soil moisture first, then the pot, then the temperature.

Should I mist a pothos with yellow leaves?

Misting does not fix wet soil. It raises surface humidity briefly and can encourage fungal spots on already stressed foliage.

How long until I see improvement?

New growth takes two to four weeks in warm conditions. Stop intervening once the soil cycle is corrected.

Final Thoughts

Fix the soil moisture cycle first; yellow leaves are a symptom of the roots, not a request for more water or more fertiliser.
