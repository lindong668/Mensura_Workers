Pests & Diseases

Mealybugs, spider mites, scale, aphids, fungus gnats, powdery mildew, root rot and leaf spots — diagnosis first, treatment second.

Each guide below is written around one concrete problem: what you can see on the plant, what is happening in the pot, and what to do next. Every page gives the full answer in its initial HTML, with a materials list, ordered steps, mistakes to avoid, routine tips and short FAQs.

Guide 1 · 7 sections

How to Get Rid of Mealybugs on Houseplants Naturally

Mealybugs hide in crevices and protect themselves with wax, so physical removal plus repeat treatment beats one spray.

Guide 2 · 7 sections

How to Kill Spider Mites on Indoor Plants: Complete Guide

Spider mites thrive in hot dry air; rinse the foliage, raise humidity, and repeat a contact treatment every few days.

Guide 3 · 7 sections

How to Treat Powdery Mildew on Houseplants

Powdery mildew is favoured by poor airflow and humid still air with dry leaf surfaces; improve airflow and remove infected tissue.

Guide 4 · 7 sections

Why Is My Plant Getting Brown Spots on Leaves?

Read the pattern first: soft dark spreading spots point to wet roots, crisp dry ones to sun, salts or dry air.

Guide 5 · 7 sections

How to Get Rid of Fungus Gnats in Soil Permanently

Fungus gnats breed in the moist organic surface layer; dry that layer and break the larval cycle, rather than chasing the adults.

Guide 6 · 7 sections

How to Fix Root Rot in Houseplants: Step-by-Step Rescue

Unpot, cut all soft dark roots back to firm tissue, repot into a fresh airy mix, and hold back on water.

Guide 7 · 7 sections

How to Get Rid of Aphids on Indoor Plants Naturally

Aphids cluster on soft new growth; rinse them off, wipe the stems, and repeat because they reproduce without mating.

Guide 8 · 7 sections

Why Are My Plant Leaves Turning Black? Causes and Fixes

Black tissue is dead tissue: cold shock and wet roots produce soft dark areas, while severe scorch produces dry black patches.

Guide 9 · 7 sections

How to Prevent Mold on Plant Soil: Causes and Solutions

Surface mould is a symptom of a constantly damp, still, organic-rich surface — not a disease attacking the plant.

Guide 10 · 7 sections

How to Treat Scale Insects on Houseplants Effectively

Scale is a sap-feeding insect under a protective shell: scrape the shells off, then treat the crawlers on a repeat cycle.

Guide 11 · 7 sections

How to Get Rid of Whiteflies on Houseplants Naturally

Whiteflies cluster on undersides of leaves and fly up when the plant is touched. Treat with leaf wiping, yellow traps and repeated sprays, and isolate the plant first.
