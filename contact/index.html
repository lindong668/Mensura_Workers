Contact Us

One email address for everything.

Write to yokok8@outlook.com. There is no form on this site, no account to create and no field to fill in.

What you can use this address for

  • Reporting a guide that is unclear, outdated or contradicted by a dated observation.
  • Suggesting a specific houseplant problem we have not covered.
  • Asking a question about a care step and how it applies to your own conditions.
  • Exercising any of the data rights described in our Privacy Policy — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, or lodging a complaint.
  • Raising a concern about a specific ad or a link on a page.

Include the page URL and, for a plant question, what you can actually see on the plant and in the pot. We reply to correspondence and then delete it; we do not add you to a mailing list. For the fastest answer to a care problem, the relevant guide and its FAQ section are usually the quickest route.
