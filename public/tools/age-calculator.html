Age Calculator

Compute exact age in years, months and days from a birth date, plus total days lived and the countdown to your next birthday. Uses real calendar arithmetic, so leap years are handled correctly.

Why exact age needs calendar logic

Age cannot be computed by dividing days by 365, because months have different lengths and leap years insert an extra day. This tool steps through years and months first, then resolves the remaining days, borrowing from the previous month when the target day does not exist yet in the current month.

Born on 29 February

Leap-day birthdays are genuinely ambiguous. In non-leap years there is no 29 February, so different jurisdictions and systems pick either 28 February or 1 March. The difference can matter for legal age thresholds — driving licences, pension eligibility, contractual capacity. This tool reports the next occurrence of 29 February where applicable.

Counting systems differ by culture

East Asian traditions historically used a system where everyone gains a year at the New Year rather than on their birthday, meaning a newborn could be reckoned as one year old. Modern legal usage in those countries has largely moved to the international birthday-based system, but you may still encounter the older reckoning in social contexts.

What the totals tell you

  • Total days — elapsed calendar days since birth, the most unambiguous measure.
  • Total months — years × 12 plus completed months; useful for infant milestone tracking.
  • Next birthday — the upcoming date and remaining days, computed by advancing the year until the date is in the future.
