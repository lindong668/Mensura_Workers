Why Is My Plant Getting Brown Spots on Leaves?

Published 2026-10-22 09:26 · Elena Marsh · Photo sources

Discrete brown spots appear on otherwise green leaves and you cannot tell whether it is water, sun, salts or a fungus. Read the pattern first: soft dark spreading spots point to wet roots, crisp dry ones to sun, salts or dry air. Work in this order; each step depends on the one before it, and skipping ahead usually means undoing it later.

Why This Works

A brown spot is dead mesophyll, and its appearance is a record of how that patch stopped receiving or holding water. Soft dark lesions mean cells collapsed while saturated — usually root hypoxia. Crisp tan lesions mean local water loss exceeded supply, from a concentrated sunbeam, dry moving air or salts drawing water out of the tissue. The distinction is diagnostic, so treating every spot as a fungus leads to the wrong intervention.

What to Avoid

The list is short because Do not spray a fungicide before identifying and its close variants cover most relapses. Each item below reverses one of the steps you just read.

  • Do not spray a fungicide before identifying the pattern.
  • Do not water more because a leaf has a spot.
  • Do not feed a plant with soft dark spots.
  • Do not move a scorched plant into even stronger sun.

Daily Care Tips

Once the immediate problem is handled, these small habits keep brown leaf spots from drifting back into the same situation. These small routines stop Why Is My Plant Getting Brown coming back without adding products.

  • Record watering dates for a month.
  • Use low-mineral water if your tap is hard.
  • Rotate the pot so one side is not always facing the beam.
  • Check the newest leaf, which reflects current conditions rather than past ones.

FAQs

Are brown spots always a fungus?

No. In homes, water quality, sun scorch and root problems are more common than a true leaf-spot pathogen.

How do I tell sun scorch?

It appears on the sun-facing side, often as a tan or bleached patch with a crisp edge.

Should I cut the spotted leaf off?

Only if it is largely dead. Otherwise trim just the dead tissue and wait for the next leaf.

Will the spots heal?

No. Dead tissue stays brown; improvement shows up as clean new growth.

Final Thoughts

Classify the spot as soft-dark or crisp-dry, check the root zone and the sun path, then change one variable.
