Methodology & Sources

This page documents the definitions and assumptions behind the calculations on Mensura.

Length

Based on the 1959 international yard-and-pound agreement: 1 inch = 25.4 mm exactly. Derived units follow exactly: foot 0.3048 m, yard 0.9144 m, mile 1609.344 m. The nautical mile is 1852 m. This site uses the international foot, not the US survey foot (1200/3937 m).

Mass

Avoirdupois definitions: 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg exactly, 1 ounce = 1/16 pound, 1 stone = 14 pounds. These are the values used throughout the weight converter, and every figure derives from the single defined pound.

Volume

US gallon = 231 cubic inches = 3.785411784 L. Imperial gallon = 4.54609 L. US fluid ounce = 1/128 US gallon ≈ 29.5735 mL; Imperial fluid ounce = 1/160 Imperial gallon ≈ 28.4131 mL. US legal cup = 240 mL.

Area

Each factor is the square of the corresponding length factor. Acre = 4046.8564224 m² (43,560 sq ft). Hectare = 10,000 m² exactly.

Data storage

Two standards are offered separately. Decimal (SI) uses powers of 1000; binary (IEC) uses powers of 1024 with the kibi-, mebi-, gibi- prefixes. The tool identifies which is in use rather than silently mixing them.

Fuel economy

MPG (US) = 235.214583 ÷ (L/100 km); MPG (UK) = 282.480936 ÷ (L/100 km). The constants derive from 100 km in miles divided by the gallon in litres.

Temperature

Conversions apply both a scale factor and an offset, since the scales have different zero points. °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; K = °C + 273.15; °R = °F + 459.67.

Time

Date arithmetic uses real calendar arithmetic, not fixed-length years, so leap years are handled correctly. Average year length for approximations is 365.2425 days (Gregorian).

Cooking

Volume-to-weight conversion uses published typical densities. Actual values vary with brand, humidity and how an ingredient is packed — flour is especially sensitive.

Review

Definitions are reviewed against the primary standards referenced above. Corrections can be sent to juju089@qq.com.
