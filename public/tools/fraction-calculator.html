Fraction Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply or divide two fractions, with automatic reduction to lowest terms. Shows the raw result, the simplified fraction and the decimal value so you can follow each step.

Why fractions beat decimals for exactness

One third cannot be written exactly as a decimal — 0.333… never terminates. As a fraction, 1/3 is exact. This is why recipes, carpentry and engineering drawings keep fractional measures: they preserve precision that decimal rounding destroys.

The four operations

MultiplyNumerator × numerator, denominator × denominator
DivideMultiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction

Common denominators

To add or subtract, denominators must match. The most efficient common denominator is the LCM of the two denominators — using the product always works but produces larger numbers that then need reducing. This tool uses the product and simplifies afterwards, which gives the same answer with less setup.

Simplification

A fraction is in lowest terms when numerator and denominator share no common factor greater than 1 — that is, when their GCD is 1. Dividing both by their GCD achieves this in one step.

Improper fractions and mixed numbers

An improper fraction has a numerator larger than its denominator (7/4). Converting to a mixed number (1¾) is often easier to visualise, but improper fractions are far easier to compute with — which is why calculators keep them internally and only display mixed form when asked.
